Hi, I'm Soyun, a designer who's always iterating on the products I design and on myself.

My path into design has been anything but linear. I studied French, found my way into tech, and eventually pursued UX. Along the way, I've learned to view unfamiliar territory as a chance to bring a fresh perspective to the table.

I see my role as bridging people and technology. I bring a people-oriented mindset, systems thinking, and a drive for efficiency to my design work. I've found that my best work happens when I genuinely care about the people I'm designing for.

Outside of work, food is my love language and movement is my anchor. You'll usually find me baking for loved ones, traveling to new places, or sweating on my mat as a 13-year yogini.
